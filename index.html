🌲 The Holy Cedars

Symbolism:

The Cedar of Lebanon is the national emblem and appears on the Lebanese flag. It symbolizes strength, endurance, immortality, and majesty.

Historical Significance:

The wood of the cedar was prized by ancient civilisations across the Middle East for its durability, aroma, and resistance to rot. It was used in the construction of temples and ships.

Biblical and Ancient Texts:

The trees are mentioned numerous times in the Bible, where they are referred to as the "Cedars of God" and were considered sacred.

⛵ The Phoenicians

Location & Origin:

The Phoenicians are ancient civilisation, centered in what is now Lebanon, who inhabited city-states along the Levantine coast with major cities including Byblos, Sidon, and Tyre.

Maritime Masters:

They were renowned as skillful sea traders and navigators, establishing an expansive maritime trade network across the Mediterranean.

Cultural Legacy:

Their most lasting and significant contribution to the world was the development of the first complete linear alphabet (around the 11th century BC), which became the ancestor of nearly all modern alphabets, including Greek, Latin, and Arabic.
